Quantum States and Superposition: A fundamental concept in quantum mechanics and, by extension, #U, is the idea of a quantum state. Quantum states, unlike classical states, exist in a superposition of possibilities. A quantum bit or 'qubit', the fundamental unit of quantum information, can represent a 0, a 1, or any combination of both. This is in stark contrast to classical bits, which can only represent a 0 or a 1 at any given time.
